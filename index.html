Garra Reachy Mini

A complete control panel for your robot — expressions, dances, native face tracking and a live camera — plus an optional AI layer that lets it listen, talk and move on its own.

Documentation on GitHub

Install it

Open your Reachy Mini dashboard, go to Applications → Discover apps, find garra_reachy_mini and press Install, then Start. That is the whole setup.

The panel is then at http://reachy-mini.local:8042/reachy — the app prints the exact URL, access token included, in its log.

Works straight away

  • Manual control — head joystick, antennas, speed and intensity
  • 85 expressions and 19 dances from the robot's own libraries, interruptible mid-move
  • Native face tracking — the SDK's YuNet tracker, no mediapipe and no extra dependency
  • Live camera — stream, snapshot, fullscreen
  • Emergency stop — one round-trip to the robot daemon, and it holds the pose rather than recentring on its own
  • REST and WebSocket API for everything the panel does

Optional, if you want it

Conversation needs an AI provider you choose, and speech needs a companion server you run on a GPU machine. Neither is required: without them the robot is still fully controllable, and the panel says plainly which pieces are missing.

Nothing leaves your network unless you configure it to. Camera frames stay on the robot, and no transcripts are written to disk.

⚠️ It moves a real robot

Keep hands, hair and cables clear of the head and antennas. Every movement is clamped to a fixed envelope, and the AI can only pick from an allowlist of named actions — never an arbitrary pose.
